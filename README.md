# GRC Portfolio: Security Operations Case Study

A governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) case study for a security operations environment at Constellis, built from public information only. It walks through a full GRC workflow: define the scope, assess risk, write policy, and map controls to a recognized framework.

This is a portfolio exercise. It does not describe any real organization's systems or controls.

## Approach

- **Scope:** security operations center monitoring, video and access control systems, incident handling, and vendor access
- **Risk rating:** likelihood and impact on a 1 to 5 scale, following the approach in NIST SP 800-30
- **Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

## Key findings

- 10 risks identified: 4 High and 6 Medium
- The highest-rated risks involve weak credentials, vendor access, phishing, and unpatched device firmware
- 6 risks are fully covered by the two policies, 2 are partly covered, and 2 are gaps (patch management and system recovery)

## Possible extensions

- Patch and firmware management policy
- Business continuity and recovery procedure
- Security awareness training requirement
- Data retention schedule

## Limitations

The scenario is hypothetical and the ratings are illustrative. Nothing here is an audit of any real organization.
