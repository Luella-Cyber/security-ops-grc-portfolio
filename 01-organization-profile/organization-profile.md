# Organization Profile: Constellis

This case study uses only public information. It does not use or reflect any internal company information.

## Overview

Constellis is a global provider of risk management and operational support services to government and commercial clients. Its portfolio of companies includes ACADEMI, Edinburgh International, International Development Solutions, National Strategic Protective Services, Strategic Social, Tidewater Global Services, and Triple Canopy. The company is headquartered in Herndon, Virginia.

## Services

- Armed and unarmed protective security
- Intelligence support
- Emergency response
- Training
- Federal facility protection. Example: in April 2025, Triple Canopy was awarded a $95 million task order for protective security officers at FDA headquarters.
